<p>
  Group exhibition on digital art and computer viruses, curated by Franziska Nori
  (<a href="http://www.digitalcraft.org/iloveyou/">digitalcraft.org/iloveyou</a>).
</p>

<p>
  The documented tour opened at the Museum für Angewandte Kunst in Frankfurt,
  23 May–13 June 2002, and continued through Berlin in February 2003, Providence,
  11 September–4 October 2004, Copenhagen, 7 October–14 November 2004, Novi Sad
  in June 2006, and Belgrade, 11–24 July 2006. Jaromil exhibited
  <em>ASCII Shell Forkbomb</em>; the recovered sources do not identify the item
  shown in Copenhagen, so the tour-wide work is not assigned to that stop by
  inference.
</p>
