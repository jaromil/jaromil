<p>
  Solo exhibition presented 3–14 November 2021 within Wall of sounds 2021
  Prélude, with Associazione Dionysus and an opening concert by Ruth Kemna. The
  documented selection included portraits based on Cage, Whitman, Bitcoin and
  Sicilian stamps.
</p>

<p>
  The Palermo municipality also records earlier Data Portraits presentations at
  OBA / Amsterdam Public Library and MACRO in Rome. Their dates, specific rooms,
  organizers and local titles remain unresolved, so no years are inferred.
</p>
