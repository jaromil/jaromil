<p>
  Time Based Text records writing as a performance. During playback, hesitations,
  corrections and changes of pace return with millisecond timing, carrying
  emphasis that a finished block of digital text normally discards.
</p>

<p>
  The software was made by Jaromil and conceived jointly with JODI. It is a
  portable, open-source implementation commissioned through IMPAKT; the research
  does not establish the commission year. It was exhibited in
  <em>Speaking Out Loud</em> at the Netherlands Media Art Institute in 2008–09.
</p>
