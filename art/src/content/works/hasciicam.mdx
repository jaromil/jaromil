<p>
  Released in 2000, Hasciicam captures a live camera image and redraws each frame
  with characters whose different densities reproduce light and shadow. Its HTML
  output could be watched in an ordinary browser on the low-bandwidth networks
  of the period.
</p>

<p>
  The software became material for installations, performances and networked
  artworks. Contributors to its code named in the artist archive include Gerd
  Knorr, Thomas Pfau, Josto Chinelli, August Black, Martin Guy, Erich Berger and
  Alessandro Preite Martinez.
