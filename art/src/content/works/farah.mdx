<p>
  <em>Farah</em> began with a 2002 journey through Palestine. The freely
  available net-art project brings together photographs, audio and writing while
  deliberately refusing the familiar image of war as its subject. It instead
  documents stories, memory and everyday forms of joy.
</p>

<p>
  The work appeared in <em>Digital Is Not Analog</em> and ORF Kunstradio's
  <em>RADIOTOPIA</em> in 2002; <em>WILL / Negotiations</em> and
  <em>Netarts.org</em> in 2003; and the three-stop <em>RealPlay</em> programme in
  2004.
</p>
